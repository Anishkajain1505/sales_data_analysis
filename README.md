# 📊 Regional Sales Analysis | Python, EDA

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Regional Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing historical sales data to identify revenue trends, evaluate product and customer performance, understand regional differences, and uncover opportunities for business growth.

The project uses data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA) and data visualization.

The goal is to help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions related to sales strategy, product performance, customer segmentation, regional investment, and profitability.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Sales teams often lack a clear, data-driven understanding of regional performance, making it difficult to identify growth opportunities and optimize resources.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- How does revenue change over time, and what seasonal patterns exist?
- Which products generate the highest revenue and profit margins?
- Which sales channels contribute the most to total revenue?
- Which states and regions perform best?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- How are revenue, cost, quantity, and profit related?
- Where can the business improve profitability and sales performance?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** — Data analysis and preprocessing
- **Pandas & NumPy** — Data manipulation and numerical analysis
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** — Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** — Exploratory data analysis
- **Excel / CSV** — Data storage and preparation

## 📂 Project Workflow

1. **Business Understanding** — Define the business problem and analysis objectives.
2. **Data Collection** — Load the sales data and supporting tables.
3. **Data Exploration** — Inspect data types, structure, distributions, and data quality.
4. **Data Cleaning** — Handle missing values, check duplicates, standardize column names, and prepare relationships between tables.
5. **Feature Engineering** — Calculate profit, profit margin, and useful date-based features.
6. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — Analyze sales trends, products, customers, channels, and geographical performance.
7. **Data Visualization** — Create charts to identify patterns and outliers.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The reference analysis highlighted the following findings:

- **Product concentration:** Products 25 and 26 were identified as leading revenue contributors.
- **Channel contribution:** Wholesale accounted for approximately 54.1% of sales, followed by Distributor at 31.3% and Export at 14.6%.
- **Geographical performance:** California emerged as a leading state by revenue.
- **Regional performance:** The West was identified as the strongest sales region in the analysis.
- **Customer concentration:** A small group of high-value customers contributed disproportionately to revenue.
- **Pricing and profitability:** Unit price showed a strong positive correlation with revenue, cost, and profit.
